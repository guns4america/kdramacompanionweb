EDRAMA COMPANION

Community Guidelines

Effective November 18, 2026.

EDrama Companion helps fans discover and discuss English-language TV shows and movies with people they choose to connect with. Friends is an optional feature. Please be considerate and help keep our community safe.

What is welcome

  • Thoughtful show recommendations, reviews and respectful disagreements.
  • Friendly conversations about TV shows, films, actors and creators.
  • Sharing only information you are comfortable letting other people see.

Not allowed

  • Threats, harassment, bullying, hate speech or targeted abuse.
  • Sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors, grooming, solicitation of minors, or any child abuse material.
  • Unwanted sexual content, graphic violence, or content intended to intimidate others.
  • Spam, scams, malicious links, impersonation, fraudulent activity or deceptive invitations.
  • Sharing another person's private information without permission, including contact details or identifying information.
  • Illegal material or content that infringes the rights of others.

Report and block

Within Friends, use Report on a user, recommendation, or shared written review and select a reason. Reports are sent privately to the developer for review and are not shown to the reported user. Use Block to end a connection, prevent new invitations and recommendations, and stop access to shared lists through that connection. Blocking can be reversed in Friends Settings; it does not automatically reconnect you.

If you cannot use in-app reporting, email guns4america@gmail.com with a description of the incident. Do not email illegal material or sensitive personal information. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

How reports are handled

We review reports and may remove abusive content, restrict or suspend Friends access, or delete accounts that violate these rules. We may escalate credible threats or child safety concerns to the appropriate authorities. We do not promise a specific response time or outcome for each report. Abuse reports may be retained as needed to investigate incidents, prevent repeated abuse, and meet legal requirements.

Privacy and children

Friends is optional, and sharing categories start disabled. Do not post sensitive information about yourself or anyone else. EDrama Companion is not designed as a social networking service for children. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation and will act on reports of suspected abuse.

Questions or safety concerns? Contact guns4america@gmail.com.
