KDRAMA COMPANION

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how KDrama Companion, the Android entertainment tracking app published by Guns4America ("we," "our"), collects, uses, shares, retains and deletes information. It applies to KDrama Companion and its website, not to the separate Gunventory app.

Summary: Basic watch tracking works locally without Google Sign-In. Google Sign-In enables optional cloud backup and Friends. All Friends watchlist and ratings sharing categories start off. You control what you share with friends. We do not sell personal data.

1. Information we collect or process

Watchlists and device preferences

The app stores dramas and movies you track, watch statuses, episode progress, rewatches, ratings, notes, favorite actors and creators, and local preferences such as streaming services, region, reminders and recommendation dismissals. These are stored on your device. When you enable Google account sync, appropriate watchlist, ratings, notes and favorites records are also synchronized to our Firebase database.

Optional Google account

When you choose Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account email address, sign-in information, and a Firebase user identifier. These records support account management, authentication, cloud backup, synchronization and Friends. You can use core local watch tracking without connecting an account.

Friends, reviews and profiles

If you enable Friends, we store your chosen display name, assigned friend code, optional profile photo, friend invitations and relationships, direct title recommendations, and written reviews you choose to create. Friends names and optional avatars can be visible to other signed-in users who access your Friends or invitation details. Your Google email is not displayed as part of your Friends profile.

Sharing controls for Watching, Plan to Watch, Completed, Ratings, Written Reviews and Favorite People are separate and are off by default. We create per-friend copies of the information only for categories you enable, and only for accepted connections. Personal episode notes are not included in the Friends sharing projections. Changing or revoking a preference restricts future access in the app, but cannot erase copies another person has already independently saved.

Device identifiers and notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations process installation IDs and push messaging tokens to help route notifications. When signed in, the app can store a device notification token linked to your Firebase user ID for friend requests, recommendations and activity alerts. Local reminder settings and Android notification permission govern supported notification features. Turning off notification permission does not necessarily stop Firebase from processing installation IDs.

Support information

When you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your inquiry. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

2. Why we process information

  • Provide watch tracking, ratings, viewing statistics, favorites, and personalized discovery.
  • Maintain optional account authentication, cloud backups and synchronization.
  • Enable friend invitations, shared profiles, selected watchlist and review sharing, and recommendations.
  • Send requested release, episode and Friends notifications.
  • Provide support, comply with applicable legal obligations, process deletion requests, and maintain service integrity.

Our current Android build does not include a dedicated third-party advertising SDK or the Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics libraries. We do not sell personal information or use your watchlists or Friends profiles for targeted advertising.

3. Services and disclosures

Google/Firebase: We use Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging and Firebase Installations to operate account-based features. Google processes associated data under its applicable terms. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security information.

TMDb: The app requests Korean drama and movie metadata, posters, cast and creator details and reported streaming availability through The Movie Database (TMDb). TMDb receives ordinary network request information such as IP address; the app does not intentionally send private watchlist notes, Friends relationships or Google account emails to TMDb. Read TMDb's Privacy Policy.

Other users: A chosen Friends display name, friend code and optional avatar help users connect, and you can explicitly disclose selected categories of viewing information to accepted friends. We do not make private watchlist notes or account email addresses publicly browsable.

Legal requirements: Data may be disclosed in response to valid legal requests or as needed for security, fraud prevention or protection of users and our service.

Links to external streaming services and websites take you to services with separate privacy practices. Our website is served using GitHub Pages and custom-domain infrastructure, which may process ordinary connection details and server logs.

4. Your choices

  • Use local tracking without Google Sign-In.
  • Turn individual Friends sharing categories on or off; all begin off.
  • Add or remove an optional Friends profile photo, and manage your display name.
  • Manage app reminder preferences, Friends notifications and Android notification permissions.
  • Manage watchlist entries, ratings, reviews, notes and favorites using app controls.
  • Export copies of your records where the app supports backup and export. Exported files remain under your control.

5. Security and retention

Local information is stored on your device. Google-linked account records are stored with Firebase. Data is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, and Firebase authentication and security rules are used to restrict access to private account records and permitted Friends data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We generally retain cloud account data while your account is active and the relevant entries remain saved. The app uses offline synchronization, so some user-initiated changes may be queued until a network connection is available. Account deletion invokes server-side cleanup for authentication, watchlists, favorite people, written reviews, Friends profiles, codes, connections, recommendations, shared projections and stored notification tokens. A successful in-app deletion also clears corresponding data on the device where deletion was performed.

Google and other infrastructure providers may temporarily retain logs, backups or installation records according to their own retention practices. Firebase documentation describes possible removal periods of up to 180 days for some installation identifiers after the applicable deletion request. We may retain limited records where legally required or necessary for security or dispute resolution. Independently exported files or copies already saved by other users may not be recoverable or deletable by us.

6. Account and data deletion

To request deletion in a supported app version: open Profile → Data & Account → Delete account and cloud data, confirm your choice and sign in with Google again. If you have removed the app or cannot use that option, you can request deletion by email. You can also request deletion of specific app data without closing the entire account; we may need to verify ownership first.
