KDRAMA COMPANION

DATA & ACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your KDrama Companion account

These instructions apply to KDrama Companion, published on Google Play by Guns4America. You can request permanent deletion of your account and associated app data even if you no longer have the app installed.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from the Google account used to sign into KDrama Companion. Use the subject KDrama Companion account deletion and ask us to permanently delete your account and app data. We may ask you to verify account ownership; never send a password or Google verification code.

Email the developer to request deletion

Email: guns4america@gmail.com

Delete directly in the Android app

  1. Open KDrama Companion and sign in with the Google account you wish to delete.
  2. Open Profile, expand Data & Account, and select Delete account and cloud data.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE, and confirm your Google account again.
  4. Once the server confirms deletion, the app removes the local saved collection on that device.

The in-app option requires a version of KDrama Companion that includes account deletion. If it is not visible, use the email method above.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase sign-in account for KDrama Companion (not your Google account).
  • Cloud watchlists, episode progress, ratings, personal notes, and favorite actors and creators.
  • Friends display name, optional avatar, friend code, invitations, connections, recommendations, shared watchlists, and written friend reviews.
  • Stored notification device tokens and Friends sharing/notification preferences.
  • Shared projections of your activity associated with your friendships.

What may remain

Content exported or copied outside KDrama Companion (such as user-saved PDF or backup files, screenshots, or information another person already saved) cannot be deleted remotely. Limited information may be retained where legally required or necessary for security or fraud prevention; if so, we'll explain the type and retention period in our response. Deletion requests do not affect your Google account or records held independently by Google or TMDb.

Request deletion of specific data

You can also email guns4america@gmail.com to request deletion of particular app data without deleting your entire account. Please identify what you want removed so we can evaluate and process your request.

Need help? Contact the same address. Return to KDrama Companion.
